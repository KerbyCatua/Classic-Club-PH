THE DETAILS THAT MATTER

Built around the everyday.

The Essential Heavyweight Tee is designed to stand on its own or sit cleanly beneath an overshirt. Its dense jersey falls away from the body, while a ribbed neck and considered sleeve length keep the proportions balanced.

Description & construction −

100% cotton jersey · 240 GSM. Relaxed unisex silhouette with dropped shoulder, ribbed crew neck, reinforced shoulder seams and straight hem. Small tonal brand label.

Fabric & care +

Machine wash cold, inside out, with similar colours. Use mild detergent. Do not bleach or tumble dry. Reshape while damp and line dry in shade. Cool iron on reverse. Follow the sewn-in care label.

Fit & measurements +

Size M garment: chest 58 cm flat, body length 72 cm, shoulder 54 cm. Measurements are approximate; allow ±1–2 cm. Compare with a tee you already like.

Delivery & returns +

Metro Manila: 2–4 business days after dispatch. Other Philippine destinations: 4–10 business days. Request eligible returns within 14 calendar days of delivery; items must be unworn with tags attached.
