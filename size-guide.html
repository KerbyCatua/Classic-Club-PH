Find your balance.

A little measuring makes a considered choice easier. Use our garment dimensions and fit notes to find your preferred silhouette.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Measure a piece you love.

All measurements below refer to finished garments, not body measurements. Lay a similar garment flat without stretching it and compare. Choose based on how you like your clothes to sit.

Dimensions are in centimetres. Allow ±1–2 cm for normal production variation. The product page takes precedence if a style has specific measurements.

Three steps to a better fit.

01 / CHEST

Keep it flat.

Lay a tee on a level surface. Measure straight across from underarm to underarm without stretching the fabric.

02 / LENGTH

Follow the line.

Measure from the highest shoulder point beside the neck to the hem. For trousers, measure the inside leg seam.

03 / PREFERENCE

Leave room for you.

Compare your favourite garment, then consider your preferred ease. Our core silhouette is intentionally relaxed.

Between sizes? Send your garment measurements and preferred fit to client services. We can help you compare before you choose.
