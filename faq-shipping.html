01 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions. Clear answers.

How do I choose my size? −

Start with the size & fit guide and compare the garment measurements with a similar piece you own. Our core fit is relaxed. Product-specific notes take precedence; ask client services if you are between sizes.

Are the pieces designed for a particular gender? −

Our core silhouettes are designed to be worn according to your own fit preference. Use garment dimensions and styling notes rather than relying only on a size label. The intended proportion is described on each product page.

Do I need an account to order? −

No. Guest checkout is represented in this design. An account keeps your order history, delivery details and saved wishlist together for a more convenient next visit.

What currency and payment options are shown? −

All prices are in Philippine pesos (PHP) and include applicable taxes. The checkout design shows illustrative card, GCash and Maya options. They are non-functional examples; no financial information is collected or processed.

Can I change or cancel an order? −

Contact client services as soon as possible with your order number. Changes or cancellations may be possible before dispatch, but cannot be guaranteed once packing or courier handover has begun. After dispatch, eligible items follow the returns process.

Will sold-out pieces be restocked? −

Availability varies by style, colour and size. A wishlist does not reserve stock. Contact client services about a specific piece or subscribe to the Club Letter for collection updates; a future restock is not guaranteed.

How do I track my order? −

A dispatch email contains the courier reference once your parcel has been handed over. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to update. Your account shows the order status, while courier tracking provides transit scans.

What if my parcel is delayed, missing or damaged? −

Check the address and tracking record, then contact client services with your order number. Weather, holidays and courier capacity can affect estimates. For damage or an incorrect item, keep the packaging and share clear photos as soon as possible so we can investigate and arrange an appropriate remedy.

03 / RETURNS & EXCHANGES

The right fit matters.

Request a return or size exchange within 14 calendar days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, unaltered and free from marks or odours, with original tags and packaging. Try garments on carefully indoors.

01 Make a request.

Contact client services with your order number, item name, reason and preferred outcome. For faults or incorrect items, include photos.

02 Wait for approval.

We will confirm eligibility and provide return instructions and the correct return address. Do not send items to the example studio address.

03 Pack and send.

Include the approved reference and use a tracked service. For change-of-mind returns, return shipping is paid by the customer. Keep the receipt.

04 Review and resolution.

Items are inspected after arrival. Eligible exchanges depend on stock. If the replacement is unavailable, we will discuss an eligible refund.

Are any items excluded? −

Items clearly identified as final sale before purchase are not eligible for change-of-mind returns. Worn, washed, altered or incomplete items cannot be accepted under that policy. These exclusions do not remove any rights or remedies available under applicable Philippine consumer law for faulty or incorrectly supplied goods.

Who pays for return and exchange delivery? −

For a change of mind or size preference, the customer pays tracked return shipping and any confirmed exchange delivery cost. If we confirm a fault or an incorrect item, we arrange or reimburse reasonable approved shipping costs. Contact us before sending anything.

04 / REFUNDS & SUPPORT

A straightforward resolution.

After an eligible return is received and approved, allow 7–10 business days for a refund to be initiated to the original payment method. Bank or wallet posting times may add to this period. A confirmation is sent when the refund is initiated.

Original shipping is not refunded for a change of mind. For confirmed faults or incorrect items, the appropriate remedy, including approved shipping costs, will be assessed in line with applicable consumer rights. We do not issue refunds to unrelated accounts.

Still need a little clarity?

Client services is available Monday–Friday, 10am–6pm PHT. Include your order number and a clear description so we can help efficiently. Contact details on the contact page are replacement-ready examples.
